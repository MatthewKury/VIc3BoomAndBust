# ================================================================
#  Boom & Bust — Character Traits
#
#  bb_bank_governor is the office marker for the Governor of the
#  National Bank: the trait itself is the record of who holds the
#  seat (no character-scope variable needed), and its icon is the
#  visible indicator on the character everywhere they appear.
#  Added/removed only by script (bb.40 appointment, dismissal
#  button, bb.43 prosecution); dead characters drop out of the
#  country's character list, which is how vacancy is detected.
#
#  bb_lion_of_the_bourse is earned permanently after 24
#  consecutive months in office with Market Health >= 70
#  (tracked in bb_governor_admin_pulse). While its holder sits
#  as governor it adds +0.2 to the acumen row on the health bar.
# ================================================================

    type = condition
    texture = "gfx/interface/icons/character_trait_icons/master_bureaucrat.dds"
